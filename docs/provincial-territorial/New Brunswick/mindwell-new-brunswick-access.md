## About
MindWell is an **online mental health and wellness platform** with classes and training resources (for example, mindfulness and well-being learning).

## Coverage
**New Brunswick** (online access)

## Who it’s for
- People in New Brunswick who want **self-guided mental wellness tools** and classes
- Anyone looking to build habits for stress, focus, and resilience

## Contact
- **MindWell website:** https://www.mindwellu.com/

## Hours
- **Online, on-demand.** (Live sessions/classes vary.)

## Languages
- **English and French**

## What to expect
- The platform offers classes and training resources you can use at your own pace (with live sessions when offered).

## Notes
- MindWell is **not** an emergency service.
- **Provincial access is unconfirmed.** As of October 2026, the Government of New Brunswick self-guided supports page no longer lists MindWell, and mindwellu.com has no New Brunswick access page. For free self-guided supports in New Brunswick, see the GNB self-guided supports page (for example, BounceBack and Bridge the gapp), or call the 24/7 NB Addiction and Mental Health Helpline at **1-866-355-5550**.

## Privacy and safety approach
MindWell is an online wellness platform, not a crisis line. Review MindWell's current privacy terms when creating an account, and use emergency/crisis supports for immediate danger.
