## About

Saskatoon Crisis Intervention Service (SCIS) is a non-profit, community-based organization, founded in 1980, that provides crisis intervention to anyone in Saskatoon who is in distress and needs immediate help. Its **Mobile Crisis Service** runs a 24-hour crisis line and responds by phone or in person.

## Coverage

**Saskatoon and area**, Saskatchewan.

## Who it’s for

- Anyone in Saskatoon who is in distress: individuals, adults, youth, families and seniors.
- Situations can include suicidal thoughts or plans, overwhelming emotions, mental health concerns, drug and alcohol use, safety worries, family or parent-teen conflict, older adults in distress, and child abuse or neglect when Ministry of Social Services Child Protection is closed.

## Contact

- **24-hour crisis line:** 306-933-6200
- **Website:** https://saskatooncrisis.ca/

## Hours

- **24/7** (phone and mobile response in Saskatoon)

## What to expect when you call

Trained, professional crisis workers answer the call, assess the situation, and plan a response. This can be support by phone or a mobile, in-person response. SCIS also partners with Saskatoon Police Service and the Saskatchewan Health Authority on the Police and Crisis Team (PACT) for emergent mental health calls. SCIS describes its services as barrier-free, non-judgmental, confidential, 2SLGBTQ+ friendly, and non-denominational.

## Notes

- For immediate danger, call **911**.
- SCIS is also a partner of the **9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline** (call or text 9-8-8, 24/7).
