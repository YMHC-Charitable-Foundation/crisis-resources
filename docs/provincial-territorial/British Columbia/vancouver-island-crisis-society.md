## About
Vancouver Island Crisis Society (VICS) provides **short-term, non-judgemental** crisis support, crisis intervention, information, and resource connections through the 24-hour Vancouver Island Crisis Line.

## Coverage
VICS serves:
- **Vancouver Island**
- The **islands of the Georgia Strait**
- Mainland communities **between Powell River and Rivers Inlet**

## Who it’s for
- People of **all ages** who need crisis support, emotional support, and/or local resource information.

## Contact
- **Crisis Line (24-hour):** 1-888-494-3888
- **Prefer to text?** Text **9-8-8** (Suicide Crisis Helpline, 24/7)

## Hours
- **Crisis Line:** 24/7

## What to expect when you call
- Short-term, non-judgemental support and help connecting to resources.
- For immediate medical or emergency danger, call **911**.

## Notes
- VICS's local **Crisis Chat** and **Crisis Text** (250-800-3806) services were **discontinued effective May 1, 2026**. VICS directs people to text 9-8-8 or call the Vancouver Island Crisis Line instead.
