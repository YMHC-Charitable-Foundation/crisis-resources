## About
The Windsor-Essex 24-hour crisis line is run by **Hôtel-Dieu Grace Healthcare** and is listed by CMHA Windsor-Essex County and the Windsor-Essex County Health Unit (WECHU) as the local line to call when you are in crisis.

## Coverage
- **Ontario:** Windsor-Essex County

## Who it’s for
- People in Windsor-Essex County who are in a mental health or substance use crisis
- People worried about someone else

## Contact
- **Call (24/7):** 519-973-4435

## Hours
- **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**

## Related services
- **Mental Health & Addictions Urgent Care Centre (ages 16+):** 1030 Ouellette Ave., Windsor. Crisis walk-in services are listed as available 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., 7 days a week. Information: 519-257-5111 ext. 77968.
- **9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline:** call or text **9-8-8**, 24/7.

## Notes
- The former Distress Centre of Windsor-Essex County line (519-256-5000) **closed on December 31, 2020**. Use 519-973-4435 instead.
- If you are in immediate danger, call **911** or go to your closest hospital emergency department.
