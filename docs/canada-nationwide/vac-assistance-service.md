## About
The [Veterans Affairs Canada (VAC) Assistance Service](https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/contact-us/talk-mental-health-professional) provides **free, confidential, short-term psychological support** from a mental health professional, available **24 hours a day, 365 days a year**.

## Coverage
- **Canada-wide**

## Who it’s for
- **Canadian Armed Forces Veterans**
- **Former members of the RCMP**
- Their **families** and **caregivers** (children up to age 21, or up to 25 if full-time students)
- You do **not** need to be receiving other VAC services, and you do not need to apply.

## Contact
- **Call (24/7):** **1-800-268-7708**
- **TTY (hearing-impaired):** **1-800-567-5803**
- **Online chat (to book an appointment):** Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time, excluding statutory holidays (see the VAC page for access steps)

## Hours
- **Phone:** 24/7, 365 days a year

## Languages
- **English and French** (responders are bilingual)

## What to expect when you call
- A Canadian mental health professional answers. They hold at least a master’s degree in a psychosocial field and are trained in crisis and suicide intervention.
- If the situation is urgent, they provide immediate support over the phone.
- They can refer you to a local mental health professional, usually with an appointment within five working days, or provide support by telephone if you live in a remote area.
- You can receive **1 to 20 hours** of support per issue. This can cover work-related issues, health concerns, family and marital problems, psychological difficulties, and bereavement.

## Notes
- If you are in immediate danger, call **911**. For suicide crisis support, you can also call or text **9-8-8**.
