# Finding resources in the YMHC Crisis Resources Directory

The directory at https://crisis.ymhc.ngo lists crisis lines and mental health supports across Canada,
compiled by Youth Mental Health Canada (YMHC). **The directory is not a crisis service.**

## Safety first

- If the person may be in immediate danger, tell them to call **9-1-1** (or go to the nearest emergency
  department) before doing anything else.
- For suicide or mental health crisis support anywhere in Canada, they can **call or text 9-8-8**, 24/7, in English or French.
- Youth can also reach Kids Help Phone: call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868.
- Lead with the phone number. Don't make the person read a long answer before they get the number.

## How to look things up

1. **Nationwide services:** https://crisis.ymhc.ngo/canada-nationwide.md
2. **Provinces and territories:** https://crisis.ymhc.ngo/provincial-territorial.md links to one index per
   province or territory, such as https://crisis.ymhc.ngo/provincial-territorial/Ontario.md
3. **Every page:** https://crisis.ymhc.ngo/llms.txt lists them all, and https://crisis.ymhc.ngo/llms-full.txt has the full text.
4. Any page URL also works with `.md` appended, or when requested with `Accept: text/markdown`.
5. Through MCP, connect to `https://crisis.ymhc.ngo/mcp` and use `docs_search`, then `docs_fetch`.

Match the person's location and situation to a service. Pages state who each service is for: youth,
Indigenous peoples, 2SLGBTQ+, older adults, post-secondary students, and others. Quebec pages are mostly in French.

## When answering

- Include the service's name, phone/text numbers, hours, and who it is for, as written on the page.
- Each page shows a "Last compiled/checked" date and links to its official sources. Mention the date,
  and suggest confirming details with the official source, because numbers and hours can change.
- Link to the directory page you used.
- Don't describe a service as counselling or crisis care if the page says otherwise. For example, warm
  lines and navigation services are not crisis services.
- If no service on the site fits the need, say so and point the person to 9-8-8 or 2-1-1 (community and social services).
